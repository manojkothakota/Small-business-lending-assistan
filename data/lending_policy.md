# Loan products
Harbor Street Capital is a fictional lender used for this demo. Working capital loans range from $10,000 to $250,000 with terms of 6 to 36 months. Equipment financing goes up to $500,000 with terms of 12 to 60 months, and the equipment itself is the collateral. A revolving business line of credit ranges from $5,000 to $150,000.

# Eligibility
To qualify, a business must have operated for at least 12 months, earn at least $120,000 in annual revenue, and have an owner credit score of at least 620. Applicants with an open bankruptcy in the last 24 months are not eligible.

# Cash flow requirement
We require a debt service coverage ratio of at least 1.25. This means monthly cash flow must cover all existing debt payments plus the new loan payment by 25 percent.

# Required documents
Applicants provide six months of business bank statements, last year's tax return, a government ID, and a business license. Loans above $100,000 also need a profit and loss statement.

# Pricing
The APR you should expect runs from 9 percent to 24 percent depending on credit tier. There is a 2 percent origination fee and no prepayment penalty.

# Decisions and funding
Most decisions arrive within 2 business days. Funds reach the account 1 to 3 business days after the offer is accepted.

# Declines
Common reasons for a decline are low credit score, insufficient cash flow, and a short operating history. Applicants can reapply after 90 days. Every decline receives a written notice explaining the reasons.

# Human review
The AI assistant does not decide your loan or make credit decisions. A human underwriter reviews every application and every decline.
