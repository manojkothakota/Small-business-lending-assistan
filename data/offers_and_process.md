# Autopay discount
Borrowers who set up automatic payments from a business checking account receive a 0.25 percentage point reduction in APR for the life of the loan.

# Excellent credit discount
Owners with a credit score of 720 or higher receive a further 0.50 percentage point reduction in APR on any approved loan.

# Relationship pricing
Loans of $100,000 or more receive a 0.25 percentage point reduction in APR when the borrower also opens a business checking account with us.

# Credit builder line
Owners below a 620 credit score can apply for a small secured line of credit of $5,000 to $15,000. Six months of on-time payments makes the owner eligible to apply for a full loan product.

# On-time payment reward
After 12 consecutive on-time payments, a borrower may request a rate review and a possible 0.25 percentage point reduction on the next loan.

# How to apply
Step 1: Run the quick eligibility check online. Step 2: Upload the required documents. Step 3: A human underwriter reviews the application within 2 business days. Step 4: Review and accept the written offer. Step 5: Funds are sent 1 to 3 business days after acceptance.
